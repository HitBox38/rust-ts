# Handoff: lane encode-contract (plan-m5 D3, wave 32, contract-first)

Branch `lane/encode-contract`, base main `c58a6fc4`. Covers four things:
- the `Encodable` codegen for every set that derives `Decodable`;
- the generic `Encoder` contract;
- the `EncodeContext` skeleton;
- a contract test that pins who owns what.

## What landed

1. **tools/codegen: `Encodable` mirrors `Decodable`.**
   - **Sets.** All 17 sets with `derive_decodable` now have `derive_encodable`. That gives 580 `encode_<T>` against 580 `decode_<T>`. The generated files are regenerated and committed (70 `.gen.ts`). Decode output is unchanged: the only `-` lines in the generated diff are encode heads and moved hunks.
   - **Encoder types.** Each derive's encoder type mirrors its decoder (serialize.json `encoders`):
     - `Encodable` takes a `SpanEncoder`;
     - `TyEncodable` takes a `TyEncoder`;
     - `MetadataEncodable` takes the `EncodeContext`;
     - `Encodable_NoContext` takes an `Encoder`.

     A per-set `encoders` override mirrors `decoders`: rustc_ty's `Encodable_NoContext` takes a `TyEncoder`.
   - **Generic types.** A generic type's `encode_<T>` is generic over the encoder (`encode_X<T, __E extends ET = ET>(self, __encoder: __E, encode_T: Encodable<T, __E>)`), like `decode_X`.
   - **serialize.json entries.** These now apply to set items on the encode side too (`serItemEntry(r, "encode")`, the new `encTemplate`), as they did for decode.
   - **No untyped closures.** An item with no encoder no longer gets type-argument closures without types: it emits `unported_encodable(v)` alone.
   - **Upstream derive names.** Upstream has only `Encodable`, `Encodable_NoContext`, `TyEncodable` and `MetadataEncodable` (rustc_macros lib.rs:81/113/120/142). There is **no** `BlobEncodable` / `LazyEncodable` derive: `MetadataEncodable` is the encode half of both `BlobDecodable` and `LazyDecodable`. So `ENCODABLE_DERIVES` is complete as it stands.
   - **serialize.json `encode` templates.** Added for every external type whose impl exists. They import from the same module as the decoder, so the generated files gain no new runtime import edge (except leaf `std/panic.ts` in four files, for the `unported_encodable` helper):
     - types generated by other sets (61);
     - rustc_span types via the `SpanEncoder` methods (`$E.encode_def_id($v)`, mirroring `$D.decode_def_id()`);
     - newtype indexes, `Align`, `Size`, `Pu128`, `IndexVec`, `Fingerprint`, `PackedFingerprint`, `Svh`, `TargetTuple`;
     - `PanicStrategy` and `SymbolManglingVersion` (hand-expanded like their decoders);
     - the 40 inline-asm register enums (`encode` added to the `def_reg_class!` / `def_regs!` helpers in rustc_target asm/mod.ts);
     - `BitMatrix`, `LazyTables`;
     - the codec.rs types (`encode_Ty`, ...; stubs, see below);
     - `Region` / `Const` (type_ir serialize.rs).
   - **Field reads.** `stable_hash_access` (the encoders' field reads) was added for:
     - `DenseBitSet` / `BitMatrix`: new `to_u64_words()` in bit_set.ts, the inverse of `from_u64_words` (D6);
     - `CrateRoot.stable_crate_id_`.
2. **The `Encoder` contract.** rustc_serialize was already complete (`Encoder`, `FileEncoder`, `MemEncoder`: 107/107, 36/36, 12/12). Two changes:
   - the container encoders in serialize.ts (`encode_Vec`, `encode_Option`, `encode_tuple`, ...; 26 fns) are now generic over the encoder (`<T, S extends Encoder = Encoder>(v, s: S, encode_T: Encodable<T, S>)`), like the decoders;
   - `TyEncoder` (rustc_middle ty/codec.ts) now `extends SpanEncoder` (upstream `trait TyEncoder<'tcx>: SpanEncoder`).

   rustc_span's `impl<E: SpanEncoder> Encodable<E>` fns take a `SpanEncoder`; the `span_encoder` cast is removed.
3. **`src/rustc_metadata/rmeta/encoder.ts`.**
   - **Class.** `class EncodeContext implements TyEncoder` has every field of encoder.rs:52 and every method of its `Encoder`, `SpanEncoder`, `TyEncoder` and two `impl EncodeContext` blocks, in upstream order. The free fns, `AnalyzeAttrState`, `EncodedMetadata`, `encode_metadata`, `with_encode_metadata_header`, the header writers, `provide`, `rendered_const` and `classify` follow.
   - **Ported here:**
     - the `Encoder` impl (`encoder_methods!`, `rustc-macro` markers);
     - `TyEncoder` (`position`, `type_shorthands`, `predicate_shorthands`, `encode_alloc_id`) and `EncodeContext_CLEAR_CROSS_CRATE`;
     - `ExpnIndex`'s `encode` (:145);
     - the lazy machinery (`emit_lazy_distance` :472, `lazy` :494, `lazy_array` :510);
     - the `LazyValue` / `LazyArray` / `LazyTable` writers (:122/:128/:137), placed in rmeta/mod.ts next to their `Decodable` impls (the generated encoders import them from the types' module, and mod.ts must not import encoder.ts at runtime);
     - `encode_LazyTables` (`define_tables!`'s `MetadataEncodable`, hand-expanded next to `decode_LazyTables`).
   - **Everything else is a stub.** A `// stub: <path>:<line> <name> (lane X)` line sits over the final signature, and the body is `unported("rustc_metadata::rmeta::encoder::… (lane X)")`. Lanes replace each stub in place with its body and a `// rustc:` marker.
   - **Not loaded at startup.** rustc_metadata/lib.ts does not import encoder.ts yet: nothing calls it, and loading it would change evaluation order. Lane encoder-d wires it when it ports `encode_metadata`.
4. **Converted `rustc-skip:` Encodable markers.** All 37 Encodable skips are converted; the other 12 of the plan's "49" are span_encoding format skips and the `FileEncoder` `Drop`, which are not Encodable impls.
   - **Ported (12):**
     - rustc_index vec.rs:372;
     - fingerprint.rs:172 and :216;
     - spec/tuple.rs:55;
     - ast tokenstream.rs:149;
     - attr_ir data_structures.rs:523 (`DocAttribute`);
     - type_ir serialize.rs:28, :62, :84, :105 and :125;
     - codec.rs:170 (`AllocId`).
   - **Stubs (25):** listed and pinned in the contract test; 23 belong to encode-middle and 2 to encoder-a.
5. **Contract test** `src/rustc_metadata/rmeta/encoder_contract.test.ts` (8 tests) checks:
   - every encoder.rs fn is ported or a stub of the lane in the split table, exactly once; the stub's `unported` text names its lane;
   - the list of hand-impl stubs outside encoder.rs;
   - `EncodeContext` has every method of its impl blocks;
   - stubs stop as UNPORTED with their lane;
   - the `Encoder` / `TyEncoder` impls (bytes; `insert_full` indices);
   - the lazy machinery against hand-computed bytes (backwards distance to the node start, forward distance between lazies, empty `LazyArray`, `LazyTable` width/len), and its three assertions;
   - round trips through `MemEncoder` / `MemDecoder` for the generated, hand-expanded and helper encoders (IntTy, Mutability, CrateType, CtorKind, PanicStrategy, SymbolManglingVersion, X86InlineAsmRegClass, TargetTuple, Fingerprint, Svh).

   tools/codegen/serialize.test.ts gained a test for the encoder type per derive, generic heads, imports and the per-set override.

## PORT decisions (in the code)

- `EncodeContext` fields that clash with a `TyEncoder` method get `_` (`type_shorthands_`, `predicate_shorthands_`).
- `FxIndexSet` fields with `insert_full` are `Map<T, index>`.
- `predicate_shorthands` keeps codec.ts's `Map<string, number>` key type.
- `metadata_hasher` is the vendor `XxHash3_128` itself; `hygiene_ctxt` is the context; `source_file_cache` is a tuple.
- `lazy` / `lazy_array` take the element's `Encodable` fn.
- `PhantomEncodable` for `Lazy*` type arguments.
- `EncodedMetadata`: the mmap is a `Uint8Array`; the temp dir is a path.
- `classify` / `Classification` are at module level.
- The hand-expanded `encode_PanicStrategy` / `encode_SymbolManglingVersion` sit next to their decoders.
- `DenseBitSet` / `BitMatrix` get `to_u64_words` (D6 inverse).
- The DocAttribute encode spells each field's `Encodable` as the SpanEncoder methods / generated encoders.
